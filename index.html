Privacy Policy for Unwind

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Unwind is an Android stretching, mobility, and wellness application intended for adults aged 18 and older. This policy explains how information is handled by the app and relevant third-party services.

1. Information You Provide

Depending on the features you use, Unwind may store optional profile information (such as name, age, gender, height, and weight), fitness preferences, goals, workout-plan answers, workout history, progress, favorites, reminder preferences, and app settings. This information supports app functionality and personalization.

2. Local Storage

Unwind primarily stores profile, workout, and preference information locally on your Android device. No developer-operated account is required, and Unwind does not upload your workout history or profile to a developer-operated server. Some information may leave your device through Google Play, Android backup services, or storage locations you select.

3. Google Play Billing and Premium

Unwind uses Google Play Billing to display Premium purchase options, process purchases, and restore purchases. Google may process purchase-related information, account or device identifiers, and diagnostic information under its own policies. Unwind uses purchase results to determine Premium access and may store a local entitlement record. The developer does not directly receive your payment card details through the app. See the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Android Automatic Backup

Depending on Android version, device configuration, and user settings, Android may back up eligible app data through Google's backup services or support device-to-device transfers.

  • Android 7–8.1: configured rules prevent eligible app data from being included in Android cloud backup.
  • Android 9–11: cloud backup is restricted to cases where client-side encryption is available.
  • Android 12 and later: cloud backup is restricted to devices with supported encryption capabilities.
  • Supported device-to-device transfers may still occur.

Certain temporary data and purchase-entitlement information are excluded from configured backups. Google and Android control the operation and retention of their backup services. Older backups created under previous app versions may remain subject to Google's policies and user settings. The developer cannot directly access your Google account backups.

5. Manual Backup and Restore

Unwind allows you to save and restore backup files in locations you select. A manual backup may include profile information, workout history, and settings.

Important: Manual backup files are not encrypted by Unwind. Anyone who obtains a backup file may be able to read its contents. If you save a backup in a cloud-synchronized folder, the storage provider may upload and process it under that provider's privacy policy. Choose a secure location and delete unwanted copies yourself.

6. Reminders and Notifications

If enabled, workout reminders are scheduled using Android's local notification and scheduling features. Unwind does not send your reminder schedule to a developer-operated notification server. You can change reminder settings in the app and notification permissions in Android settings.

7. Advertising, Analytics, and Servers

Unwind does not include third-party advertising or a separate third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK. It does not require a developer-operated user account or upload workout history or profile information to a developer-operated server. Google Play, Android, and device-level services may independently handle technical or diagnostic information.

8. Third-Party Services and Sharing

Unwind does not sell your personal information. Relevant external services may include Google Play Billing for purchases, Android and Google backup services for eligible backups and transfers, and storage providers you select for manual backups. Their own privacy policies and settings apply.

9. Retention and Deletion

Local app information generally remains until you edit or delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. To remove locally stored information, use Android Settings > Apps > Unwind > Storage > Clear storage. This does not necessarily delete manual backup files, cloud-synchronized copies, Google account backups, or Google Play purchase records. Manage those copies through the relevant provider or account settings. The developer cannot directly delete information stored only on your device or in your personal backup accounts.

10. Security

Unwind uses Android's app storage protections and configured backup restrictions. No electronic storage or transfer method is completely secure. Manual backup files are not encrypted by Unwind. Protect your device with a secure screen lock and keep backups in trusted locations.

11. Children's Privacy

Unwind is designed and intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children through developer-operated services. For concerns about a child's information, contact us using the address below.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as app features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The updated version will appear at this page with a revised date when appropriate.
